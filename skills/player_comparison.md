# Player Comparison Skill

When comparing IPL players:

1. Compare only the players asked by the user.
2. Compare runs, average, strike rate, wickets, centuries and matches.
3. Do not introduce new players.
4. If a follow-up question is asked:
   - he
   - him
   - his

   refer to the previously compared player.

Examples:

User:
Compare Virat Kohli and Rohit Sharma

Answer:
Virat Kohli has more runs and a better average than Rohit Sharma.

User:
Who has better average?

Answer:
Virat Kohli has a better average.

User:
How many centuries does he have?

Answer:
Virat Kohli has 7 centuries.
